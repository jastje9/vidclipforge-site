Local-first creator workflow

Gaming highlights.
Ready to edit.

VidClipforge is a desktop application for turning gaming stream recordings into short vertical video drafts. Identify notable moments, generate subtitles, apply a video layout, and review clips before sharing.

Explore workflowSupport & documentation

Built for stream highlights

Create short-form clips from your own or appropriately licensed/authorized gaming recordings.

Highlight discovery

Uses audio activity, speech transcription and optional visual analysis to suggest interesting sections to review.

Vertical editing

Exports 9:16 video drafts with themed layouts, readable titles, source attribution and subtitles.

Publishing workflows

Manages exported drafts and optionally connects accounts for uploads when a platform authorizes that integration.

How it works

01

Choose a source

Open a local recording or add an authorized livestream source. Choose a segment or a processing mode.

02

Review draft clips

The desktop software analyzes media, proposes highlight timestamps and renders short video drafts for review.

03

Export or publish

Preview the finished MP4 files. Only connect social accounts or submit content through officially supported authorization routes.

Desktop-first by design

Media stays in your workspace

Core editing runs on the user's computer. Files are uploaded only when a user enables an integration or explicitly performs a publishing action.

Respect creators and platforms

Users must have rights to record, edit and republish media. AI highlight scores are suggestions rather than a guarantee of accuracy, virality or earnings.
