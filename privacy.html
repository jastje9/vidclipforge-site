Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026 · Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy describes how VidClipforge, a desktop video-clipping application, handles information when you use the desktop software and optional integrations. This document describes the current intended desktop-first workflow; features depend on your installed version.

1. Information handled by the app

Depending on what you use, VidClipforge processes media files or authorized livestream segments, speech transcripts, highlight timestamps, generated titles, exported clips and related metadata. If you connect a platform such as YouTube or TikTok, the app may receive OAuth access and refresh tokens and basic account information needed to authorize uploads.

2. Where processing happens

Core video/audio processing and most project data are stored locally on your computer. If you explicitly configure an external AI service, a remote endpoint may receive media samples or transcript excerpts to generate suggestions. Optional integrations can send content and metadata to the services you connect.

3. Platform integration and purpose

Authorized account information and tokens are used to connect accounts, check permissions and submit videos or drafts only as supported by the enabled integration. TikTok and YouTube process information under their own privacy policies. We do not sell personal information.

4. Access tokens and security

Platform credentials are intended to be stored in the user's local application environment rather than published in this website or repository. Keep your computer secure. Never share client secrets, access tokens, refresh tokens or Google OAuth JSON files in a public repository.

5. Retention and deletion

Local media and drafts stay on your computer until you remove them or uninstall and clean up the application's data directory. You can revoke third-party account access using the relevant platform's account security settings. Revoking platform access does not automatically remove content already uploaded to that platform.

6. Website analytics

This static informational website does not include custom tracking scripts, analytics pixels or marketing cookies. GitHub Pages, which hosts the site, may process standard web request information under GitHub's policies.

7. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell user data. Information may be transferred to a platform or processing service only when the user configures and uses that connection. We may need to comply with applicable legal obligations.

8. Your rights and questions

You may ask questions about the app's handling of information or request assistance with locally stored data through Support. Avoid including passwords, access tokens, or private personal information in public support requests.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as features evolve. A revised effective date will be displayed on this page.
